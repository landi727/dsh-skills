# Character and Narrative Illustration

## Analyze

- Record the proportion system for head, torso, limbs, hands, feet, and repeated characters.
- Determine recognition-bearing silhouette, facial cues, hair, clothing, props, color coding, and controlled exaggerations.
- Record line of action, pose balance, joint simplification, gesture clarity, gaze, expression, and interaction.
- Identify the narrative beat, character roles, causal chain, and the graphic devices that connect actions or scenes.
- Compare character treatment across focal, supporting, distant, and inset figures.
- Record how head, face, hair, clothing, limbs, and props receive value planes, highlights, shadows, material cues, turning lines, and detail at each character tier. Follow `object-modeling.md`.
- Record body/prop overlap, limb crossings, foreground interruptions, face and gesture visibility, and local front/back reversals. Follow `layering-hidden-shapes.md`.
- Separate style rules from the source character's identity, outfit, pose, and story.

## Transfer and prompt

Map the target character's identity cues into the reference's proportion, silhouette, facial-detail, gesture, clothing-simplification, layer-role, visibility, modeling-mode, and object-detail-density systems. Preserve face, gesture, action readability, and role hierarchy. Derive new poses and interactions from the target narrative.

Follow generation-blueprint-schema.md for prompt order. Promote narrative beat, focal action, gaze or expression when they define this reference; attach remaining character-tier and modeling controls locally. Keep character count within the semantic-group and canvas-density budget. Do not add facial gradients, glossy clothing, anatomical turning lines, or prop reflections without source support.

## Accept

Check identity readability, proportion consistency, silhouette, limb and prop occlusion, protected face and gesture visibility, modeling mode, facial and clothing value-plane count, material response, line of action, hand and face abstraction, object-detail density, gaze, interaction, role hierarchy, and narrative clarity at thumbnail size. Treat imported source poses, outfits, or identities as content leakage.
