# Technical and Information Illustration

## Analyze

- Identify projection convention, visible faces, scale system, section, cutaway, exploded relation, or diagram topology.
- Record line codes for outline, hidden edge, axis, dimension, leader, motion, and annotation.
- Record labels, numbering, arrows, legends, callouts, units, and reading order.
- Record whether labels, leaders, cutaways, insets, windows, and exploded parts sit above, behind, or clipped inside the main diagram; protect their connection and legibility. Follow `layering-hidden-shapes.md`.
- Separate exact information from decorative styling and inferred geometry.
- Check whether visual simplification preserves functional relationships and part identity.
- Separate information-bearing projection and construction lines from line-based volume. Record allowed turning, thickness, and perspective lines plus part-detail density through `object-modeling.md`.
- Record hierarchy between main diagram, supporting views, notes, and navigation devices.

## Transfer and prompt

Preserve required facts, labels, units, connections, sequence, layer roles, clipping containers, and protected visibility. Follow generation-blueprint-schema.md for prompt order; exact factual requirements retain priority over conflicting inferred styling. Preserve projection, diagram relationships, modeling mode, line-volume functions, and part-detail hierarchy while translating color, typography, panel, and motif treatment. Keep decorative and material effects subordinate to information.

Place required text and data in a separate exact-content block. Mark unresolved technical facts rather than inventing them.

## Accept

Check factual relationships, part count, projection, connection, sequence, layer roles, clipping and cutaway boundaries, label/leader visibility, line-volume roles, value-plane limits, part-detail density, label-to-object mapping, arrows, legends, text accuracy, and information hierarchy before judging decorative style. Treat visually attractive misinformation or unsupported depth embellishment as a failure.
