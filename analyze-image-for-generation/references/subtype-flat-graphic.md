# Flat Graphic Illustration

## Analyze

- Determine whether recognition comes from silhouette, color-block partition, outline, internal geometry, symbols, or typography.
- Record primitive families, curve and corner behavior, geometric regularity, intentional deformation, and shape economy.
- Separate flatness from mechanical precision; identify hand-shaped deviations or registration behavior.
- Map the hierarchy between silhouette contours, borders, internal divisions, and fill-defined edges.
- Record opaque cover, shared boundaries, knockouts, inset panels, clipped scenes, contour termination, and outline redraw. Follow `layering-hidden-shapes.md`.
- Record the object-modeling mode, supported value-plane count, gradient policy, lighting ceiling, material response, line-based volume, clean-mass share, and object-tier detail progression. Follow `object-modeling.md`.
- Record palette roles, hue-distance pattern, temperature allocation, value ladder, adjacency rules, large clean planes, discrete side planes, and symbolic shadows. Follow `color-relationships.md`.
- Determine how typography, badges, icons, or panels integrate with the image rather than treating them as added copy.

## Transfer and prompt

Translate each target into a strong silhouette plus a small set of recognition-bearing internal cues. Preserve the reference's primitive grammar, edge hierarchy, opaque-overlap grammar, visibility priorities, color-block proportions, clean-plane allocation, allowed deformation, value-plane limit, and lighting/material ceiling. Specify which hues are fixed and which palette relationships may vary.

Place geometry, modeling mode, value-plane rule, lighting ceiling, focal structure, density budget, and color-block allocation before material-rich nouns and decorative motifs. Keep continuous gradients, glossy highlights, reflections, ambient occlusion, extra perspective lines, realistic material rendering, and unstructured microdetail out when the reference does not support them.

## Accept

Check silhouette recognition, primitive consistency, layer roles, overlap type, contour termination, protected visibility, contour hierarchy, modeling mode, value-plane count, highlight/shadow footprint, material ceiling, line-based depth, object-detail density, clean-plane area, color-block adjacency, large-role separation at thumbnail size, value separation in grayscale, symbolic volume, typography integration, and preservation of the selected focal and canvas-density structure. Treat generic vector cleanliness as insufficient when the reference depends on controlled irregularity.
